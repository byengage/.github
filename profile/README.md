## Products · منتجات منظومة انجيج

### 1. [Engage Decks · انجيج للعروض التقديمية](https://engagedecks.com)
Interactive presentations built Arabic-first (RTL) with full English support. Generate a complete slide deck with live polls, timed quizzes, word clouds, audience Q&A, and idea walls from a short description, or import existing PowerPoint and PDF files. Edit slides in natural language with **Engage AI**, export editable 16:9 PowerPoint slides, and let attendees join from any phone browser via QR or session code with no app and no sign-up.

### 2. [Engage Posts · انجيج للمنشورات](https://engageposts.com)
AI social media content studio for businesses, online stores, restaurants ([دليل المطاعم](https://engageposts.com/ar/restaurants), [دليل المتاجر](https://engageposts.com/ar/stores), [دليل الأسر المنتجة](https://engageposts.com/ar/families)), and creators. Turn a single idea or daylight phone product photo into branded motion reels, studio product shots, multi-size posters, carousels, platform-ready post copy, and UGC-style talking ads with AI presenters in Saudi, Gulf, Levantine, Egyptian, Standard Arabic, or English, with unified scheduling and **Success compass** analytics.

### 3. [Engage Chats · انجيج للمحادثات](https://chats.byengage.com)
Smart inbox that sorts incoming customer messages and drafts replies in your tone strictly from a business guide you control. Turn on **Cover for me** when busy to answer routine inquiries automatically with deterministic code guardrails that never invent prices, links, or promises, plus a public contact link (`chats.byengage.com/@byengage`) and built-in local B2B lead discovery.

### 4. [Engage Calls · انجيج للاجتماعات](https://calls.byengage.com)
Live translated browser video meetings for cross-language teams, traders, and international suppliers. Everyone speaks their own language and hears and reads others in theirs (including Saudi, Gulf, Egyptian, Levantine, and Standard Arabic), with original text shown underneath every translation, guest access via browser link with no app download, zero audio recording, and mutually confirmed bilingual meeting minutes.
