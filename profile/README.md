## Products · منتجات منظومة انجيج

### 1. [Engage Decks · انجيج للعروض التقديمية](https://engagedecks.com)
Interactive presentations built for Arabic (RTL) and English. Generate a complete slide deck with live polls, timed quizzes, word clouds, audience Q&A, and idea walls from a two-line prompt, or import PowerPoint and PDF files. Edit slides in a sentence with **Engage AI**, and let attendees join from any phone browser via QR or session code with no app or sign-up.

### 2. [Engage Posts · انجيج للمنشورات](https://engageposts.com)
AI social media content studio for businesses, stores, and creators. Turn a single idea into branded motion reels, cinematic montages, multi-size posters, carousels, platform-ready post copy, and UGC-style talking ads with AI presenters in Saudi, Gulf, Levantine, Egyptian, Standard Arabic, or English, with properly shaped right-to-left Arabic typography and unified scheduling.

### 3. [Engage Chats · انجيج للمحادثات](https://chats.byengage.com)
Smart inbox that sorts incoming customer messages and drafts replies in your tone from a business guide you control. Turn on **"Cover for me"** when busy to answer routine inquiries automatically with strict guardrails that never invent prices, links, or promises. Email works today, with social channels coming soon, plus a public contact link (`chats.byengage.com/@name`) so customers can write without needing a phone number.

### 4. [Engage Calls · انجيج للاجتماعات](https://calls.byengage.com)
Live translated browser video meetings for cross-language teams, traders, and international suppliers. Everyone speaks their own language and hears and reads others in theirs (including Saudi, Gulf, Egyptian, Levantine, and Standard Arabic), with original text shown underneath every translation, guest access via browser link with no app download, and mutually confirmed bilingual meeting minutes.
